# ConstructIQ — Sales Pitch & Commercial Strategy

**Category:** AI-powered Construction Data Intelligence Platform

## Executive Summary

ConstructIQ is an enterprise SaaS platform engineered to solve the $30 Billion annual inefficiency problem in Indian real estate and construction. It unifies fragmented site data, detects schedule/budget risks early using an explainable 0–100 risk engine, and automates material procurement and reporting.

---

## Core Value Proposition

### 1. What is it?
ConstructIQ is an end-to-end site data management and operational intelligence platform for construction companies and real-estate developers. It converts raw daily logs, spreadsheets, and material receipts into actionable risk alerts, procurement orders, and executive reports.

### 2. What problem does it solve?
- **Cost Overruns:** 70% of Indian construction projects experience cost overruns due to delayed visibility into material price hikes and contractor delays.
- **Material Stockouts:** Site work halts frequently because steel or cement inventory dips below safety thresholds without warning.
- **Reporting Overhead:** Project managers spend 10+ hours per week manually compiling Excel reports for management.

### 3. Who is it for?
- **Primary Target:** Mid-sized real-estate developers building residential high-rises, commercial complexes, and industrial parks (₹10 Cr – ₹200 Cr project portfolios).
- **Secondary Target:** General Contractors (GCs) and Project Management Consultancies (PMCs).

### 4. Why buy it?
- **25% Reduction in Project Delays:** Early warning risk alerts allow PMs to fix contractor delays before they hit critical paths.
- **Zero Stockout Downtime:** Automated low-stock procurement triggers ensure materials arrive 48 hours before floor slab casting.
- **10x Faster Executive Reporting:** Automated weekly PDF report generation in 1 click.

### 5. What makes it different?
Unlike generic ERPs or heavy US-centric tools like Procore, ConstructIQ is tailored for Indian real-estate workflows (Indian currency formatting in ₹ Cr / Lakh, WhatsApp/CSV import center, role-based mobile views, and transparent rule-based risk engines).
